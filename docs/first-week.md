# First Week Guide

Welcome to the team! This guide walks you through your first week as an intern.

## Day 1: Laptop and tooling setup
- Collect your laptop and sign in with your company account.
- Install the core tooling: git, a terminal, an editor (VS Code is the team default), and the languages used by your team.
- Set up your SSH keys and authenticate with GitHub.
- Clone this repository and confirm you can open it locally.

## Day 2: Orientation
- Read this guide end to end.
- Skim `docs/glossary.md` so team vocabulary doesn't slow you down.
- Walk through `docs/onboarding-checklist.md` for the full overview.

## Day 3: Meet your mentor
- Have your intro meeting with your mentor.
- Agree on a first small task and where to ask questions (team chat, office hours).

## Day 4-5: Ship a hello-world PR
- Create a branch, add a file with your name and a short hello message, open a pull request.
- Watch the "Onboarding CI" workflow run manually to see what CI looks like here.
- Get a review, merge it, and celebrate your first contribution.

## Where to get help
- Your mentor is the first stop.
- Team chat for anything that feels too small to be a formal question.
- No question is too basic in week one - ask early, ask often.
